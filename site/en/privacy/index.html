2. What we process and why

Event enquiry form

If you submit the form, we process your name, email address, phone number (if given), the planned date, number of guests, type of event, and the message you write.

  • Purpose: answering your enquiry, preparing a quote, and the contact needed for that.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). If the enquiry does not lead to a contract, further retention rests on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the correspondence traceable.
  • Retention: 1 year from the close of the enquiry. If a contract is concluded, accounting records are kept for 8 years as required by Hungarian law.

The "Message" field is free text. Please do not enter health data there (allergies or dietary restrictions, for example) — we will discuss those by phone or email while planning. If you do enter such information, we use it solely to run your event and delete it together with the enquiry.

Phone and email enquiries

If you call or write to us, we process your contact details and the content of the enquiry — for the same purpose, on the same legal basis, and for the same period as above.

Website statistics

We measure traffic with Simple Analytics. It uses no cookies, builds no visitor profiles, and collects nothing that could identify you personally. We only see aggregate figures: how many people visited, which pages, and roughly where they came from. The data is stored within the European Union.

3. Cookies and data stored in your browser

This site sets no marketing or tracking cookies, which is why there is no cookie banner.

  • Language choice: when you switch language, we store that choice in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so you do not have to set it again. It never leaves your device and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser data.
  • Map: the Google map loads only if you click "Show the map". Until you do, Google receives nothing from you. Once loaded, Google acts as its own controller, may set cookies and will see your IP address — governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Fonts: the site's fonts are served from our own server, so loading them sends no data to any third party.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data or pass it on for marketing. We use the following providers, acting as processors:

  • Netlify (Netlify, Inc., United States) — website hosting and form submission handling. Transfers to the US rest on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision, with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as the fallback mechanism.
  • Google Workspace — the restaurant's email account, where form submissions arrive.
  • Simple Analytics — aggregate traffic statistics, no personal data, stored in the EU.

Beyond that, we may disclose data where a legal obligation requires it.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request:

  • access — to know what data of yours we hold;
  • rectification — if anything is inaccurate;
  • erasure — where there is no longer a purpose or legal basis;
  • restriction — suspension of processing in certain cases;
  • portability — the data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • objection — against processing based on legitimate interest.

Write to flexnfresh2023@gmail.com for any of these. We reply within one month at the latest.
